Orbix AI is a visual command center for your apps plus a smart assistant. A live particle core sits in the middle of the screen and every app you connect orbits around it.
You can connect messengers and channels such as Telegram, Bale, Eitaa and Rubika. Each one needs a bot token and a channel ID.
The Profile page shows your account and how many apps are active, deactive, disconnected or unreachable. From it you can open the Status page for details of each app.
The Settings button (gear icon, top right of the home screen) holds the app options such as music.
The assistant only answers questions about using Orbix AI.
